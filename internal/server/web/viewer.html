Use your phone as the remote

  1. Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi as this computer.
  2. Open your phone's Camera to scan the QR code.
  3. Tap the link that pops up. The remote opens on your phone.
No Wi-Fi? Turn on Personal Hotspot on your phone (Settings → Personal Hotspot), then connect this computer to it. The code updates by itself.
